    The road to the platinum in five stages, in order. Built only from the official trophy descriptions — no mission steps are invented, because the game launched today and no verified walkthrough exists yet. Each stage lists its trophies; the bar shows your progress in that stage.

    Missable rule: a trophy is flagged missable when its description ties it to a specific story mission, a story moment, or a one-time condition. All other trophies can be earned after the story. Mission replay has not been confirmed for GTA 6, so treat every flagged trophy as “do it when it comes up”.

    Your next steps, in roadmap order: the earliest unearned trophies first, with the count trophies you are part-way through. Earn these before wandering off — the missable ones come first.

      About this guide

      All 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned; hidden flags here follow PSN. The list has no online trophies — every trophy can be earned in single player, so the Online filter is empty on purpose.

      Guides are written in our own words from the official descriptions. The game launched today; where a place or step is not pinned down by the description, the guide says so instead of inventing it. Each trophy cites its sources beside its guide.

      Sources: Exophase — GTA VI trophy list; PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophy list. Fully offline: this page fetches only its own data file and makes no network request.
